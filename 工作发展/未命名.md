# 🎯 完整面对面对话稿（自然口语版）

---

## 🟢 0️⃣ 开场 + 简单自我介绍

**你：**

> Hi Dr Nguyen, nice to meet you in person.  
> My name is Jiajie Ye — I’m currently doing MSc Artificial Intelligence.

如果你想更自然一点：

> I’m JIajie Ye, MSc AI student. I’ve been looking forward to meeting you.

---

## 🟢 1️⃣ 礼貌询问称呼

接着自然问：

> By the way, how would you prefer me to address you? Dr Nguyen, or Tuyen?

（他如果说叫 Tuyen，你以后就直接叫 Tuyen。）

---

## 🟢 3️⃣ 简述职业方向 + 相关经历

> In terms of my future direction, I’m quite career-oriented.  
> I’d like to work as an LLM or agent engineer after graduation.

> Recently I’ve been building some agent-related systems — things like multi-agent orchestration, tool-calling, structured reasoning — so I’m particularly interested in system-level LLM applications.

简洁即可，不要讲太细。

---

## 🟢 4️⃣ 提到项目选择

> Among the project options, the one that feels most aligned with my interests is _Large Language Model in Smart House Environments_.

> But I’d really like to hear your thoughts — do you think this would be a good fit for me, or is there another project you would recommend?

停一下，让导师说话。

---

## 🟢 5️⃣ 简述你对项目的理解

> From my understanding, this project is about using an LLM to take high-level user instructions and decompose them into low-level executable actions in a smart home environment.

> So it’s essentially an LLM-based planning and orchestration system.

---

## 🟢 6️⃣ 询问项目核心重点

> I was wondering — from your perspective, what would be the main focus of this project?  
> Would it be more about evaluating LLM reasoning, or more about building a robust system architecture?

---

## 🟢 7️⃣ 提出你的初步想法（自然表达）

> I’ve also been thinking about possible extensions.  
> For example, currently it seems mostly text-based. I was wondering whether it might be interesting to support multimodal input — like speech, or even simple visual signals — by adding a multimodal encoder before the planning stage.

停一下。

> Of course, these are just early thoughts — I’d really appreciate your guidance on whether that would be within scope.

语气一定要温和。

---

## 🟢 8️⃣ 轻松闲聊：就业市场

自然过渡：

> Since I’m thinking quite seriously about my career path, I’m also curious — from your experience supervising students, how do you see the current AI job market across different countries?

> Are there particular technical directions that seem especially strong or in demand right now?

如果想更轻松一点：

> Do you think LLM-based agent systems are a strong direction for industry in the next few years?
